## Platform

web

Surfaces: a browser extension (overlay badges on listing pages plus a side panel/popup) and a desktop web app. Mobile is out of scope for now.

## Product

Shaker is a trust and price layer for Croatian second-hand marketplaces: Njuškalo, Facebook Marketplace, Index oglasi and Vinted. Users leave reviews on individual listings ("oglasi") and sellers. Shaker scrapes comparable listings to find better prices, helps buyers negotiate, checks the seller's history, and checks whether messages from the other side are consistent with the listing (scam patterns, payment-link tricks).

## Users

Buyers and sellers, weighted equally.

- **Buyers** browse a listing and want to know: is the price fair, is the seller trustworthy, can I haggle, does this message look like a scam.
- **Sellers** are reviewed subjects and want to build and protect their reputation, respond to reviews, and price their listings competitively.

The product must stay fair to both sides: a listing or seller with no data is unknown, not suspicious.

## Language
